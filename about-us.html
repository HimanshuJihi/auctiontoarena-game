About Us

Auction to Arena ek krantikari 3D cricket game hai jo aapko sirf cricket khelne ka hi nahi, balki apni team banane ka bhi pura maza deta hai. Yeh game cricket fans ke liye banaya gaya hai jo strategy aur action dono pasand karte hain.

Game Features (Visheshatayein)

  • Realistic IPL Auction: Is game ka sabse bada feature iska Auction system hai. Aap apni pasandida team (jaise MI, CSK, RCB) chunte hain aur phir real-time bidding mein hissa lete hain. Aapke paas ek budget hota hai jisme aapko best players khareed kar apni squad banani hoti hai.
  • 3D Gameplay Experience: Auction ke baad, aap apni banayi hui team ke sath maidan mein utarte hain. Game mein high-quality 3D graphics hain. Aap batting mein lofted shots aur defensive shots khel sakte hain. Bowling mein aap swing, spin, aur pace variations ka istemal kar sakte hain.
  • Tournament Mode: Sirf ek match nahi, balki pura IPL season khelein. Points table par nazar rakhein, matches jeetein, aur apni team ko playoffs aur finals tak lekar jayein.
  • Advanced Community Blogs: Yeh sirf ek game nahi, ek community hai. Yahan aap cricket news padh sakte hain, apne blogs likh sakte hain, aur unhe polls aur YouTube videos ke saath aur bhi engaging bana sakte hain.
  • Social Interaction: Apni profile banayein, dusre users ko follow karein, unke posts par like aur comment karein. 24-ghante ki stories post karein aur private messages ke zariye dosto se judein. Apne achievements ke liye 'Badges' bhi jeetein!
  • Challenges & Practice: Apni skills ko test karne ke liye 'Challenge Mode' khelein jahan aapko Super Over chase ya wickets bachane jaise scenarios milenge. 'Practice Mode' mein aap bina pressure ke batting aur bowling seekh sakte hain.
  • Creator Monetization & Studio: Ab apne passion ko profession banayein! Hamare Creator Studio ke zariye apne video views par real money earn karein. Apni kamai ko seedhe apne UPI ya Bank Account mein withdraw karein.
    (Turn your passion into a profession! Earn real money based on your video views via our Creator Studio. Withdraw funds directly to your UPI or Bank Account.)
  • Profile Badges & Privacy: Apne blogs aur engagement ke basis par special badges jeetein jaise 'Verified User', 'Popular Author', aur 'Community Pillar'. Sath hi, aapke followers ki list ab puri tarah private hai.
    (Earn special badges based on your engagement. Plus, your followers and following lists are now completely private and secure to you!)
  • Video Ads & Banners: Platform par seamless VAST Video Ads aur Banner Ads ka support hai, jo creators ki earning badhane mein madad karta hai.
    (Experience seamless VAST Video Ads and Banner Ads integration, designed to help creators boost their earnings while providing a premium platform experience.)
